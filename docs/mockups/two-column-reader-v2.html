◎Assistant

For a single example, cross-entropy compares the target distribution with the probabilities predicted by your classifier:

L(y,p)=−∑k=1Kyklogpk
Click to preview⌕

If the target is one-hot, only the log-probability of the correct class contributes. This equation stays next to the explanation that introduces it.

The chain rule then connects the loss, logits, hidden activations, and trainable weights. Here is the expanded expression:

∂L∂Wij=∑n=1N∑k=1K∂L∂pk(n)∂pk(n)∂zk(n)∂zk(n)∂hi(n)∂hi(n)∂Wij

In practice, autodiff computes these products for you. The separate factors are useful for diagnosing saturation and exploding gradients.

∂L∂zk=pk−yk
